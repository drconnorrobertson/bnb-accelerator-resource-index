Short-term rental research glossary

Understand gross revenue, net operating income, cash flow, cash-on-cash return and underwriting assumptions.

Published by My BnB Accelerator, LLC · Company-owned resource
Home / Short-term rental research glossary

Gross revenue

The top-line income before expenses. A projection should say whether cleaning charges, guest taxes and other pass-through amounts are included. Two revenue numbers cannot be compared fairly until their definitions match.

Net operating income

Revenue less operating expenses, before loan payments and income taxes. Ask which costs are included. A model that omits repairs or management can appear stronger without describing a better property.

Cash flow

For the worksheets in this resource collection, operating income less annual debt service. Cash reserves and major capital work need their own treatment. A positive projection does not establish future cash receipts.

Cash-on-cash return

Annual pre-tax cash flow divided by the cash invested. The denominator matters: a figure based only on the down payment will differ from one that includes closing, setup and reserves.

Occupancy and average daily rate

Occupancy measures booked nights as a share of available nights. Average daily rate measures room revenue per booked night. Owner use and blocked nights affect how these terms should be interpreted.

Evidence versus assumptions

Evidence documents an observation, such as a dated statement or written quote. An assumption estimates a future value. Keep both visible so a reviewer can distinguish recorded history from a forecast.
