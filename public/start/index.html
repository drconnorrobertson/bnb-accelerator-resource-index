Where to start with BNB Accelerator

A research path for understanding BNB Accelerator and preparing to evaluate a property.

Published by My BnB Accelerator, LLC · Company-owned resource
Home / Where to start with BNB Accelerator

If you are researching the company

Start with the company’s service explanation. Write down which tasks are included, who performs them, and which commitments appear only in marketing. Follow with the current written proposal: it should describe the fee, milestones, deliverables and termination provisions for your situation.

Read customer feedback separately. A review describes an experience; it does not establish what every future buyer will receive. Check dates, specific services and whether the reviewer discusses a completed acquisition or an earlier stage.

If you are reviewing a property

Create an evidence folder before comparing projected returns. Include the listing, documented operating history where available, a dated expense schedule, financing terms and written permission checks. Record a source and date beside every major assumption.

  • Separate purchase price from total cash required. Include closing, furnishing, initial repairs and reserves.
  • Compare the projected nightly rate and booked nights with properties similar in size, location and amenities.
  • Ask what happens if revenue falls while insurance, maintenance or financing costs rise.
  • Confirm local requirements directly with the relevant authorities and review property-specific restrictions.

If you are ready to speak with the team

Bring your available cash, timing, preferred level of involvement and open questions. Ask for the current scope in writing. Keep a copy of the proposal alongside the consultation checklist so you can compare the conversation with the actual agreement.
